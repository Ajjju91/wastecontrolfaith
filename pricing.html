Pricing FAQs

Q: Do we need to commit long-term?

No — audit engagements are project-based. Retainer agreements are typically annual with quarterly review options.

Q: Can we switch models later?

Absolutely — many clients start with a fixed audit, then move to retainer governance once savings are identified and implemented.

Q: What if we don't find significant savings?

Rare, but if audit reveals no material opportunities, we'll tell you and discuss next steps honestly.

Q: How is "verified savings" calculated in the success fee model?

We calculate baseline from audit, document implemented changes, and measure recurring savings via invoice and KPI tracking over 12 months.
