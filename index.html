Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What does a waste audit cost?

We offer a complimentary scoping call. Audit fees depend on site size and complexity; many audits pay for themselves within 3–9 months from discovered savings.

Q: How quickly will we see savings?

Quick wins (rightsizing, frequency changes) typically show within 1–3 months. Contract and program changes compound savings over 6–12 months.

Q: Do you handle hazardous or WEEE waste?

Yes — we include hazardous and WEEE in our stream analysis and recommend licensed handling and compliant disposal routes.

Q: How do you measure success?

We set a baseline in the audit and measure monthly against KPIs (cost per tonne, diversion rate, missed collections, invoice error rate).

Q: What engagement models do you offer?

We offer fixed-price audits, retainer governance (monthly), or success-fee models where we share verified savings.

Q: Can you work with our existing vendors?

Yes — we often renegotiate with current suppliers or recommend consolidation. We work within your existing contracts or support transitions.
