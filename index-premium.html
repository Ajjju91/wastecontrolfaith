Waste Performance KPIs & Governance

Data-driven metrics for cost, operations, and ESG performance

Waste is often one of the least controlled operating expenses in commercial real estate. Most portfolios lack visibility into waste spend, composition, vendor performance, and ESG impact. Our approach uses industry-standard KPIs to turn waste into a transparent, measurable business function.

Measurement enables cost savings, operational efficiency, and sustainable ESG performance.

💰 Financial KPIs

Cost per Tonne Managed

Definition: Total waste cost ÷ total tonnage managed (annual or monthly)

Why it matters: Reveals pricing efficiency and overbilling. Industry benchmarks range $40–$120/tonne depending on waste streams and disposal methods.

Client value: Identify overpaying vendors, negotiate better rates, and validate cost reductions month-over-month.

Benchmark target: 15–30% reduction in cost per tonne through optimization.

Year-over-Year Cost Reduction

Definition: Annual waste spend reduction compared to prior year

Why it matters: Tracks program effectiveness and sustainability of savings. Without active governance, waste costs typically increase 8–15% annually.

Client value: CFO-level metric showing program ROI and ongoing savings delivery.

Benchmark target: 20–25% cost reduction in Year 1; sustained improvement in subsequent years.

Invoice Accuracy Rate

Definition: % of invoices with no billing errors, duplicate charges, or unauthorized services

Why it matters: Studies show 15–30% of waste invoices contain errors. Even 5% error recovery equals significant savings.

Client value: Recover 5–15% of annual waste spend through invoice audits alone.

Benchmark target: 98%+ invoice accuracy with monthly reconciliation.

Cost Savings per Site

Definition: Average monthly or annual waste cost savings per location

Why it matters: Enables portfolio-wide comparison and identifies underperforming locations.

Client value: Scalable insights for multi-site optimization and benchmarking.

Benchmark target: $500–$5,000+ per site monthly, depending on portfolio size.

⚙️ Operational KPIs

On-Time Collection Rate

Definition: % of scheduled pickups completed on time (within contract hours)

Why it matters: Missed collections disrupt site operations and create overflow issues. Indicates vendor performance and reliability.

Client value: Improves operational continuity and enables SLA-based vendor accountability.

Benchmark target: 95%+ on-time collection rate.

Missed Collection Rate

Definition: % of scheduled pickups missed or delayed beyond contract windows

Why it matters: Each missed collection creates operational disruption, tenant complaints, and potential compliance violations.

Client value: Vendor scorecards with performance-based pricing adjustments.

Benchmark target: Less than 5% missed collections; escalate vendors exceeding threshold.

Route Efficiency

Definition: Collections per route, tonnage per pickup, trucks per week

Why it matters: Inefficient routes drive unnecessary hauling costs. Right-sizing frequency and consolidation yields immediate savings.

Client value: Reduce operational overhead through optimized collection schedules.

Benchmark target: Reduce collections per week by 15–30% through frequency optimization.

Vendor Performance Scorecard

Definition: Composite score of reliability, accuracy, responsiveness, and compliance

Why it matters: Objective vendor evaluation drives accountability and informs contract renewals.

Client value: Use scorecards to negotiate better rates and improve service.

Benchmark target: Maintain vendors scoring 80%+ or transition to alternatives.

🌍 Sustainability & ESG KPIs

Waste Diversion Rate

Definition: % of waste diverted from landfill through recycling, composting, or recovery

Why it matters: Core ESG metric required by investors, regulators, and sustainability initiatives. Reduces landfill disposal costs.

Client value: Strengthen ESG reporting and meet investor sustainability expectations.

Benchmark target: 40–70% diversion rate; industry leaders exceed 75%.

Recycling Contamination Rate

Definition: % of recycling stream contaminated by non-recyclables

Why it matters: High contamination reduces recycling facility efficiency and can result in rejection of entire loads.

Client value: Improve program effectiveness and vendor relationships through contamination reduction.

Benchmark target: Below 5% contamination rate; industry best practice under 2%.

Carbon Impact of Waste Services

Definition: Estimated tonnes CO₂ equivalent from waste transport, processing, and disposal

Why it matters: Scope 3 emissions metric increasingly required for ESG disclosures and climate commitments.

Client value: Support carbon reporting and demonstrate progress toward sustainability goals.

Benchmark target: Reduce carbon per tonne through diversion and vendor optimization.

Waste Stream Composition

Definition: % breakdown of general waste, recyclables, organics, hazardous waste

Why it matters: Composition data drives optimization strategies and program design.

Client value: Identify opportunity streams (organics, cardboard) for diversion programs.

Benchmark target: Baseline audit followed by targeted reduction of high-impact streams.

🛡️ Compliance & Risk KPIs

Regulatory Compliance Score

Definition: Composite assessment of adherence to local, state, and federal waste regulations

Why it matters: Non-compliance risks fines, facility shutdowns, and legal liability.

Client value: Reduce regulatory risk and audit readiness.

Benchmark target: 100% compliance score; zero violations.

Documentation & Manifest Accuracy

Definition: % of waste disposal manifests accurately completed and filed

Why it matters: Hazardous waste manifests are legal documents; errors create liability.

Client value: Audit readiness and reduced legal exposure.

Benchmark target: 100% manifest accuracy with quarterly audits.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are cost savings typically achieved?

Through four primary channels: (1) invoice error recovery (5–15% of spend), (2) service optimization like frequency adjustment (8–12%), (3) vendor renegotiation (5–10%), and (4) waste stream improvements (2–5%). Most clients see results within 3–6 months.

Do you replace our waste vendors?

Not necessarily. We assess your current vendors against performance and pricing benchmarks. If vendors are underperforming, we renegotiate terms or recommend alternatives. Many clients keep existing vendors after renegotiation.

How is waste data collected and verified?

We combine multiple methods: invoice audits, site assessments, direct vendor data feeds, and digital tracking. This multi-layer approach ensures data accuracy and transparency.

Who benefits most from waste governance programs?

Commercial real estate owners, REITs, retail chains, industrial facilities, healthcare systems, and corporate campuses with $500K+ annual waste spend and multiple locations. Larger portfolios see proportionally higher savings.

What's the timeline to see results?

Quick wins (service optimization, invoice recovery) typically show within 1–3 months. Full program maturity and sustained savings develop over 6–12 months. ROI is usually achieved within the first year.

Do you support ESG reporting?

Yes. We establish waste diversion, carbon, and sustainability metrics aligned with ESG frameworks (SASB, GRI, TCFD). These metrics are reportable to investors and support sustainability commitments.

What's included in ongoing governance?

Monthly invoice validation, KPI tracking, quarterly reviews, vendor performance scorecards, and executive dashboards. We act as your dedicated waste governance partner.

What's the investment for a waste governance program?

Program costs vary by portfolio size and complexity, but typically range from $15K–$50K annually. Most clients achieve ROI within 6–12 months through cost savings alone.
