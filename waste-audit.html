Waste Audit: Understand Before You Change

A comprehensive site-level examination of all waste streams, composition, volumes, collection frequency, and billing. Uncover hidden costs and quick-win opportunities.

How We Conduct Your Waste Audit

1

Scoping & Data Request

We request manifests, invoices, and service frequency data to understand your current program baseline.

2

Site Visit & Composition Study

We sample bins and weigh representative loads to produce a waste composition profile (paper, food, plastic, hazardous, etc.).

3

Invoice Reconciliation

We match service frequency and container size to billed items and identify divergence and billing errors.

4

Opportunity Mapping

We identify quick wins (e.g., resizing bins, adjusting pickups), medium-term wins (recycling streams), and long-term structural savings.

5

Report & Roadmap

A clear, costed action plan with ROI for each recommendation and a 12-month savings forecast.

Common Quick Wins We Find

Typical savings we uncover in most audits

✓ Right-sizing containers

Reducing oversized bins to match actual waste volume — often saves 15-25% on disposal charges immediately.

✓ Optimizing pickup frequency

Converting frequent small pickups to fewer, larger collections — reduces hauling costs and carbon footprint.

✓ Introducing segregated recycling

Separating recyclables and organics to avoid landfill surcharges and capture recycling credits.

✓ Removing duplicate services

Identifying and cancelling inactive or redundant contracted services still being billed.

✓ Correcting billing errors

Identifying overbilled items, duplicate charges, and incorrect rates — typical recovery of 5-10% of annual spend.

✓ Vendor consolidation

Negotiating better rates by consolidating fragmented contracts with fewer, larger vendors.

Audit FAQs

Q: How long does an audit take?

Typically 3-6 weeks from data collection to final report, depending on portfolio size and complexity.

Q: Can you audit during COVID or access restrictions?

Yes — we adapt by using site photos, manifest review, and remote analysis when on-site visits aren't possible.

Q: What if we have multiple waste streams?

We analyze all streams: general waste, recycling, organics, hazardous, WEEE, confidential, construction, etc.

Q: Will the audit disrupt operations?

Minimal disruption — we schedule visits during low-activity times and coordinate with your team.
